AIVista — AI Search Revenue Agent

AIVista is a search intelligence and revenue decision-making platform using AI technology aimed at assisting brands in knowing how they perform on AI searches.

Unlike other search intelligence tools that analyze search rankings, AIVista analyzes recommendations by AI assistants, competitor mentions, sources referenced, and places where visibility is lost.

AIVista uses an agentic investigation approach where:

Website
   ↓
Website Analysis
   ↓
Customer & Category Understanding
   ↓
High-Intent Query Generation
   ↓
AI Search Testing
   ↓
Competitor Analysis
   ↓
Citation Intelligence
   ↓
Visibility Gap Diagnosis
   ↓
AI Recommendation
   ↓
Experiment
   ↓
Measure Impact
Core Capabilities
AI Search Visibility Analysis — Measure how frequently a brand appears in AI-generated answers.
High-Intent Query Generation — Generate buyer-focused queries based on the company's products, category, and target customers.
AI Response Analysis — Analyze AI-generated responses and identify brand and competitor mentions.
Competitor Intelligence — Identify which competitors are recommended instead and understand their visibility advantage.
Citation Intelligence — Analyze the sources and domains that influence AI-generated recommendations.
Visibility Gap Diagnosis — Identify topics, queries, and customer intents where the brand is underrepresented.
AI-Powered Recommendations — Convert findings into actionable experiments and content/product recommendations.
Revenue Opportunity Analysis — Connect AI-search visibility gaps with potential business and revenue opportunities.
Human-in-the-Loop Decisions — Keep humans involved before recommendations are turned into experiments.
Technology

Frontend: React, TypeScript
Backend: Node.js, Express
Database: Firebase Firestore
AI: Google Gemini
AI Architecture: Agentic AI / LLM workflows
Search & Intelligence: AI Search APIs, Web Crawling
Visualization: Interactive dashboards and charts
Deployment: Cloud-based deployment

Product Philosophy

AIVista is built around a simple product loop:

Investigate → Diagnose → Recommend → Experiment → Measure

The goal is not simply to show whether a company was mentioned by an AI assistant, but to explain why competitors are being recommended, what evidence supports the finding, and what action the business can take next.

Example Investigation

An investigation can progress through:

Analyze the company's website
Identify its product category
Identify target customers
Generate high-intent queries
Test those queries across AI assistants
Analyze competitor mentions
Investigate citation patterns
Diagnose visibility gaps
Generate and prioritize an experiment

The existing investigation interface implements this nine-stage workflow, including website analysis, query generation, AI-response testing, competitor analysis, citation investigation, diagnosis, and recommendation synthesis.

AIVista turns AI-search observations into actionable revenue intelligence.
